What is Revamp365?+
Revamp365 is an AI-native all-in-one SaaS platform for real estate investors. It combines property data, MLS and off-market search, skip tracing, CRM, SMS, AI voice agents, deal analysis, offers, and automations in one system — so you don't need a PropStream + CRM + dialer + chatbot stack.
What does it replace?+
Separate subscriptions for data (PropStream, BatchLeads), CRM (Podio, REsimpli), dialers, skip tracing vendors, SMS platforms, and AI tools — consolidated into one login.
Who is it for?+
Wholesalers, flippers, landlords, and acquisition teams who want faster deal flow without juggling disconnected tools or hiring VAs for manual follow-up.
Is there a free plan?+
Yes. Start free with $5 in credit — no card required. Use the credit toward data, skip traces, SMS, or AI calls to test the platform before upgrading.
Why AI-native?+
AI is built into the core — voice agents, deal analysis, lead qualification — not bolted on as a third-party add-on. As AI improves, so does the whole stack.
